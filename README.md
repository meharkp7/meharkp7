## 🛰️ Quanta

### *when the road changes, the route should think.*

A dynamic routing intelligence system built for **SIH26137**.

It combines optimization, graph learning, forecasting and reinforcement learning to adapt routes to changing transportation conditions.

**under the hood**

`QPSO` `GNN` `Transformer`  
`PPO` `SUMO` `PyTorch`

<td width="50%" valign="top">

## 🕵🏻 Aegis

### *from suspicious traces to structured intelligence.*

A security intelligence platform designed around **evidence, attribution and entity resolution**.

The backend is built to transform fragmented threat information into structured, traceable intelligence.

**under the hood**

`FastAPI` `PostgreSQL`  
`Python` `Docker` `NLP`

<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

## 🐝 Swarm2Creative

### *what if AI didn't have to work alone?*

An experimental multi-agent platform where specialized agents collaborate across **art, music, stories and architecture**.

Instead of one model doing everything → multiple agents, each with their own role.

**under the hood**

`Multi-Agent AI` `LLMs`  
`FastAPI` `WebSockets` `React`

<a href="https://github.com/meharkp7/Swarm2Creative">

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/EXPLORE%20PROJECT-FFE5EF?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=C08497"/>

</a>

</td>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

## 🧠 NEXUS / InsightOS

### *giving telemetry a brain.*

A federated hybrid-telemetry architecture for transforming application signals into **context-aware insights** while keeping privacy in the loop.

Featuring a Ghost Instrumentation Layer, contextual tagging, local buffering and PII-aware privacy controls.

**under the hood**

`AOP` `Telemetry` `Privacy`  
`PII Masking` `Feature Engineering`

</td>
</tr>

<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

## 🌍 GeoAI Vision

### *teaching machines to see the planet.*

Computer vision workflows for high-resolution geospatial imagery using **SAM/LANSAM-style segmentation**, spatial tiling and georeferenced outputs.

From pixels → masks → actual geographic information.

**under the hood**

`SAM` `LANSAM` `PyTorch`  
`Rasterio` `GeoPandas` `GDAL`

</td>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

## 💡 EquiBond

### *technology for impact.*

A blockchain-based Social Impact Bond platform exploring how smart contracts can connect **funding, outcomes and accountability**.

**under the hood**

`Ethereum` `Solidity`  
`Web3` `Smart Contracts` `Stripe`

</td>
